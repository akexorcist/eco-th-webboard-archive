"How to Draw Heads"
This is a brief 5-step tutorial on how to draw anime style heads.



First off, start with a circle. Then you make a cross right under the bottom end of the circle. Draw the cross the way you want, i.e. if you want a 3/4 view like head, then draw them like in this picture, if you want to draw a front viewed head, then draw a cross from the front (think in 3D, then I'm sure you'll know what I mean).


Now you have your cross. What to do next? Draw in the face (how to make the face look nice? Learn from reference or look forword to my next tutorial, where I'm gonna explain how to draw nice faces ^-^).

Right, now a little note on the eyes: draw the eyelids "doubled" (as i always say ^-^), that means that you shouldn't draw the outline of the eye as a simple straight line, but like here in this picture. Good, again when you draw the face think in 3D, the more often you do that, the better and quicker you'll get.


^-^. Other than that, keep on practicing and look at other pictures for reference, then you'll know how to draw the outline of the face sooner or later.

The actual "trick" here is to draw the outline of the head's shape, too. although it might look stupid, it doesnt matter, but it helps you to learn the shape of the head in different positions as well as it'll help you to draw the hair correctly.


Next comes the hair. How to draw hair? Dunno o.O, but if someone requests it, I'll make a tutorial for that one. Don't expect anything great though, cause I'm no good at drawing it.


Fill out the eyes somehow. Maybe you wanna add a reflection on the hair now, too, cause if you're planning to CG your picture, it'll help you out a lot :)
