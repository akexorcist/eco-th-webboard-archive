3.1 ถ้ามี สเตตัสอะไรและทำไรได้บ้าง

• Short-range evasion +1
• Long-range evasion +1
• HP+2

Magic Transistor

• Magical on-target hit +2

Power Transistor
• Attack power +2
• Short-range on-target hit +1
• Long-range on-target hit +1

Restore Transistor

• Magical recovery power +2

Magic Defense Transistor
• Magical defense +2
• Magical resistance +2

Critical Transistor
• Critical +2
• Critical evasion +2
